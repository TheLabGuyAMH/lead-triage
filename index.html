A thousand messages after the freeze. One truck plan.

A small plumbing shop wakes up to voicemails, texts and web forms in English and Spanish, and many of them are the same job. Muse Ember reads every one, finds the ones that belong together, and quotes the words that prove it. Code fills today's trucks. Anything doubtful becomes one question for you.
