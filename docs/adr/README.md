## Safety script wording (exact, code-owned in `src/lib/safety.ts`)

Recorded 2026-11-26. Anthony accepted it as hackathon demo wording the same day (SUM.md); a licensed professional should review it before any real-customer use.

- Gas (EN): "If you smell gas, leave the building now. Don't use light switches, phones or flames inside. Call 911 or your gas company from outside. We'll call you as soon as you're safe."
- Gas (ES): "Si huele a gas, salga del edificio ahora. No use interruptores de luz, teléfonos ni llamas adentro. Llame al 911 o a su compañía de gas desde afuera. Le llamaremos en cuanto esté a salvo."
- Carbon monoxide (EN): "If your carbon monoxide alarm is sounding or you feel dizzy, sick or get a headache indoors, leave the building now and get fresh air. Call 911 from outside. Don't go back in until responders say it's safe. We'll call you as soon as you're safe."
- Carbon monoxide (ES): "Si suena su alarma de monóxido de carbono o se siente mareado, enfermo o con dolor de cabeza adentro, salga del edificio ahora y respire aire fresco. Llame al 911 desde afuera. No vuelva a entrar hasta que los servicios de emergencia digan que es seguro. Le llamaremos en cuanto esté a salvo."
- Electrical / fire (EN): "If you see sparking, smoke or fire, or smell burning wiring, leave the building now if it's not safe to stay. Don't touch the panel, outlet or appliance, and don't use water on an electrical fire. Call 911 from a safe place. We'll call you as soon as you're safe."
- Electrical / fire (ES): "Si ve chispas, humo o fuego, o huele a cableado quemado, salga del edificio ahora si no es seguro quedarse. No toque el panel, el enchufe ni el aparato, y no use agua en un fuego eléctrico. Llame al 911 desde un lugar seguro. Le llamaremos en cuanto esté a salvo."
